\# Operations Guidelines



Operational systems should generally remain within established normal ranges.



Large deviations in temperature or pressure can indicate unstable operating conditions. Persistent deviations should be investigated before they affect system performance.



Downtime should be monitored because increasing downtime can reduce operational efficiency and may indicate maintenance or reliability problems.



Throughput should be evaluated together with quality metrics. High throughput alone does not indicate good performance if quality decreases.



When abnormal conditions are detected, operators should review recent measurements, identify variables outside normal ranges, and determine whether maintenance or process adjustments are required.
