\# Incident Response Guide



When an operational anomaly is detected, the first step is to confirm that the measurement is valid.



The analyst should compare the observation with recent historical data and determine whether similar events occurred previously.



Potential contributing variables should then be identified using statistical analysis or predictive models.



If the predictive model indicates elevated risk and operational metrics also show deterioration, the case should receive additional attention.



The final incident report should include relevant measurements, analytical evidence, model results, and supporting documentation.
